# Creator kit

Everything you need to sign and onboard your first creators.

## 1. Outreach message (DM / WhatsApp)

Personalise the first line for each creator. Attach the demo video.

> Hi {Name}! {One genuine line about their content, e.g. "Your gym-motivation reels got me through last month."}
>
> I'm building a small app where fans can get a personal moment from you, in their own room: you say their name and wish them happy birthday, good luck in exams, etc. It's made from **real clips you record once** (no AI voice, no deepfake). Fans pay $4.99 and **you get 50% of every sale**.
>
> Here's a 10-second demo: {link}
>
> It takes about 30 minutes of recording, and you approve every occasion. Could I show you in a 15-minute call this week?

## 2. Interview script (15–20 minutes)

Ask about what has already happened, not about the future. Listen more than you pitch.

1. How often do fans ask you for shoutouts, birthday wishes or personal messages? What do you do with those requests?
2. What do you sell to fans today (Cameo, Topmate, subscriptions, merch)? Roughly how much does it earn a month?
3. Have you ever done paid personal videos? Why did you stop, or why not?
4. *(Show the demo.)* What's your first reaction?
5. What would worry you about this? (Reputation, AI, fans misusing it, time.)
6. Which occasions would you be happy to offer? Which never?
7. **The commitment ask:** "Would you record the clips with me on {date} and post the link in your Story once?"

**Signals that count:** a yes with a date, a clip session booked, an intro to another creator.
**Signals that don't:** "sounds cool", "let me know when it's ready", likes.

## 3. Recording guide (about 30 minutes)

**Setup**
- Phone on a tripod, portrait (vertical), 1080p, 30 fps.
- **Whole body in frame**, head to feet, with some space above and below.
- Plain, uncluttered background (a wall). A green screen is great but not needed.
- Soft, even light from the front (a window or ring light). No strong backlight.
- Quiet room; a clip-on mic if possible.
- Same outfit and position for every clip, so clips join smoothly.

**What to record**
1. **Name clips:** "Hey {Name}!" with a big smile, about 1.5 seconds each.
   Record the **top 150–200 first names of your audience** (we'll make the list from your region and audience). Pause one second between names; we'll cut them apart.
2. **Generic fallback:** "Hey you!" or "Hey superstar!", for names you haven't recorded. Record 2–3 versions.
3. **Occasion clips:** 5–12 seconds each, one per approved occasion. For example:
   - Birthday: "Happy birthday! Have an amazing year, and I hope all your wishes come true!"
   - Exam luck: "Good luck in your exams! You've got this, I believe in you."
   - Workout: "Let's go! Push hard today, I'm proud of you!"
4. Look slightly to the side of the camera at the start of each clip (as if spotting the fan), then at the lens.

## 4. Consent and agreement checklist

*Not legal advice. Have a lawyer turn this into a proper agreement before scaling past the pilot.*

- [ ] Creator is 18+ and verifies identity (ID + live selfie video).
- [ ] Creator reads a consent statement on camera: "I, {name}, agree to Moments using these clips of me for personal fan videos, within the occasions I approve."
- [ ] License scope: only personal fan moments; only approved occasions; no other use, sale or AI training without written permission.
- [ ] Revenue share: 50% of net (after payment fees), paid monthly.
- [ ] Creator can withdraw any time: new sales stop within 24 hours, and their clips are deleted within 30 days.
- [ ] All videos are labelled "AI-assisted" and carry the creator's handle.
- [ ] Report and takedown process explained; abusive orders are refunded and blocked.
- [ ] Creator's posting commitment: at least one Story with the link during the pilot.
